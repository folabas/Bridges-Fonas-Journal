About the Journal

Bridges Integrative Journal of Science and Technology (BIJST)

Bridges Integrative Journal of Science and Technology (BIJST) is a peer-reviewed, multidisciplinary scholarly journal dedicated to advancing innovative research across the sciences and technology. The journal provides a rigorous, reputable, and timely platform for the dissemination of high-quality original research, review articles, short communications, case studies, and technical reports that address contemporary scientific and technological challenges.

BIJST fosters interdisciplinary collaboration by publishing research that bridges traditional disciplinary boundaries and promotes the integration of knowledge across engineering, medicine, computing, mathematics, natural sciences, and the social sciences. The journal is committed to promoting scientific excellence, research integrity, innovation, and evidence-based solutions that contribute to sustainable development and societal advancement.

The journal welcomes submissions from researchers, academics, industry professionals, policymakers, and postgraduate students worldwide.

Our Aim

Advancing Knowledge & Innovation

The primary aim of BIJST is to provide an international platform for the publication of original, high-quality, and impactful research that advances scientific knowledge, technological innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Interdisciplinary studies that integrate multiple scientific disciplines are particularly encouraged.

Submission Guidelines
Manuscript Requirements

Submissions should be original, scientifically sound, and written in clear English. Follow the journal template with abstract, keywords, intro, methodology, results, discussion, and references.

Peer Review Process

All manuscripts undergo editorial screening, followed by double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers before the editorial decision.

Submission Process

Submit manuscript in MS Word, cover letter, declaration of originality, ethical approval, and conflict of interest statement through the portal or email.

Information for Authors

Before Submitting

  • The work is original and not under review elsewhere.
  • All authors have approved the submission.
  • References are complete and correctly formatted.
  • Figures and tables are of publication quality.
  • Funding information disclosed and conflicts of interest declared.
  • Ethical approval has been obtained where necessary.

Types of Articles Accepted

Original Research Review Articles Short Communications Case Studies

All manuscripts must be written in English.

Policies & Information

Article Processing Charge (APC)

To sustain high editorial standards, BIJST charges an APC of ₦40,000 or US$100 for accepted manuscripts.

  • There is no submission fee.
  • APCs are payable only after formal acceptance.

Privacy Statement

Names and emails are used exclusively for editorial processes. Reviewers' identities remain confidential.
